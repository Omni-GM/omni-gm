# Omni-GM

The language of a game master who runs tabletop roleplaying campaigns: what happens at the table, and what the game master needs at prep time.

## World and story

**World**:
The game master's own persistent setting. Several campaigns play out in the same world over the years.
_Avoid_: Setting, universe

**Campaign**:
A long series of sessions with one group of players in the world, carried by one main plot.
_Avoid_: Game, chronicle

**Adventure**:
A self-contained story inside a campaign, a few sessions long, with clear stakes, a clear timeframe, and a finale.
_Avoid_: Arc, module, chapter

**Main plot**:
The story that spans a whole campaign and that its adventures move forward.
_Avoid_: Metaplot, overarching story

**Adventure frame**:
The game master's intent for the current adventure: its goal, stakes, timeframe, pressure, and finale, and how it moves the main plot forward. It is intent, not story state.
_Avoid_: Adventure plan, outline, arc

**Thread**:
An open story element that the players could follow: a lead, a promise, an unanswered question.
_Avoid_: Hook, loose end, plotline

**Faction**:
A group or power in the world with its own goal, which acts whether or not the players are present.
_Avoid_: Organization, power group

**Off-screen move**:
Something a faction does between sessions while the players are elsewhere, together with its effect on the world.
_Avoid_: Background event, downtime action

**Clock**:
A track that counts down or fills up toward an event, either shown to the players or kept by the game master.
_Avoid_: Timer, countdown, counter

**Pressure**:
The urgency that pushes the players toward the main plot, usually from a clock or a threat, alternating with moments of release.
_Avoid_: Tension, stakes

**World entry**:
A lasting page for one NPC, place, or faction of the world, kept apart from any single session.
_Avoid_: Wiki page, codex entry, lore note

**World fact**:
Something the table established as true about the world that does not belong to one NPC or place, such as a custom, a law, or a past event.
_Avoid_: Lore, canon

**Story state**:
The current truth of the world and the campaign as of the last session: open threads, NPCs as they were played, places, world facts, clocks, faction positions, and character goals. It records what happened, not what the game master intends for the main plot or the next adventure.
_Avoid_: Campaign state, world state, canon

**Secret**:
Background the game master has decided but the table has not yet seen, such as who a villain really was. It is the game master's intent, open to change at any time, and becomes story state only once it is revealed at the table or the game master declares it fixed.
_Avoid_: Lore, hidden fact, GM canon

**Fizzle**:
A campaign losing momentum until it stalls: players no longer see progress, and the game master loses the energy to prep it.
_Avoid_: Burnout, campaign death

## People

**Player character**:
A character played by one of the players.
_Avoid_: PC, hero

**Character goal**:
What a player wants for their player character, which the game master ties into the story.
_Avoid_: Player plot, personal quest, backstory hook

**NPC**:
A character the game master plays, either prepared ahead or invented at the table.
_Avoid_: Non-player character, side character

## Sessions

**Session**:
One meeting of the group at the table.
_Avoid_: Episode, game night

**Prep**:
The game master's work between two sessions to get ready for the next one.
_Avoid_: Preparation, planning

**Session note**:
The game master's document for one session: the prep before it, and the notes and summary after it.
_Avoid_: Session log, prep doc

**Recording**:
The audio captured during a session.
_Avoid_: Voice memo, audio log

**Transcript**:
The verbatim text of a recording, with speakers attributed.
_Avoid_: Log, protocol

**Scene**:
One continuous part of a session, in one place and situation. Sessions break down into scenes, and what changes in the story state is tied to the scene where it happened.
_Avoid_: Encounter, beat

**Session summary**:
A scene-by-scene digest of what happened in a session, focused on the plot points that matter for the story.
_Avoid_: Recap, minutes

**Debrief**:
The step after a session that turns what happened at the table into changes to the story state. Each change carries the scene it happened in and why, and all changes from one session are recorded together.
_Avoid_: Import, sync, update

**Basis**:
Where a story-state change comes from: narrated by the game master, said by an NPC, a rumor, a player guess, a player plan, or the game master's own answer. It keeps facts apart from claims and guesses.
_Avoid_: Source type, confidence

**Frame talk**:
The conversation in which the AI asks, grounded in the story state, and the game master writes or updates the adventure frame. It runs when a new adventure starts, or briefly when the briefing flags frame fields that the last session put in question.
_Avoid_: Planning session, frame interview

**Briefing**:
The one-page, plot-first digest that opens prep, generated from the story state and the adventure frame: where the party stands, the threads that pull, the NPCs in play and what they want, the clocks and pressure, and a few off-screen moves or complications to pick from.
_Avoid_: Dossier, report, digest

**Prep sparring**:
The part of prep after the briefing: the AI walks the game master's own prep checklist, opening each section with the facts that matter for it and offering pitches, and assembles the session note from the game master's decisions only.
_Avoid_: Co-writing, prep assistant

**Pitch**:
A short idea the AI offers during prep, such as a scene, a twist, or an off-screen move, built on named story-state facts and framed as a situation, never an outcome. The game master picks, reshapes, or ignores it; an ignored pitch leaves no trace.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, proposal, draft

**Plank question**:
One targeted question the AI asks during prep when the main plot has a gap in the story state. The answer becomes a secret, unless it corrects what the table already saw.
_Avoid_: Backfill, lore interview

**Recap**:
The opening text the game master reads to the players at the start of a session to remind them of the story so far.
_Avoid_: Summary, previously-on

**Strong start**:
The opening scene that drops the players straight into action at the start of a session.
_Avoid_: Cold open, hook

**Encounter**:
A prepared fight: its opponents, their stat blocks, and the place it happens.
_Avoid_: Combat, battle

**Reward**:
Loot and items the players can gain in a session.
_Avoid_: Treasure, drops

**Idea**:
A loose thought for the campaign that comes to the game master between sessions, before it has a place in prep.
_Avoid_: Note, inspiration
