Here is my checklist, in order, in my own words:

1. Launch: the strong start. Give exactly two opening situations, never an outcome, close to where the last session ended.
2. Discoveries: ten numbered, tweet-short secrets and clues in the Lazy DM style that the characters could learn next session. Small truths I can drop wherever they fit.
3. Supplies: a mechanical section for the props and stat blocks I already have ready. No pitches. Keep only what I supply.

My markdown session-note template is:

```markdown
# Next session of The Tallow Mill
## Launch
## Discoveries
## Supplies
```

Store this once with the reason "Use my prep process in every client". Then walk Launch.
