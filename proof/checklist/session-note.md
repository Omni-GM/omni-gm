# Next session of The Tallow Mill
## Launch
Water on the cellar stairs. At first light the party opens the cellar door and finds river water on the bottom steps that wasn't there yesterday. The wheel overhead has almost stopped. Widow Ferrant stands at the top of the stairs and won't come down. In the wet stone at the waterline is a small scratch mark, like the one on the loft latch.
## Discoveries
1. The loft latch was scratched by a boat hook, the iron kind barge crews carry.
2. The wheel turns slowly because something is wedged in the millrace under the cellar floor.
3. Corwin owes Dace twelve silver from dice at the river tavern. It's a small debt and an old one.
## Supplies
- A river map
- Two apprentice tokens
